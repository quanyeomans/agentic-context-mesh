# engagement-epsilon

Temporal-reasoning engagement. Four sessions over six fictional months in which speakers refer to past and future events using relative date language — "last sprint", "the meeting two weeks ago", "before the Q1 review", "by end of next month". The corpus exercises the temporal-reasoning subscore of the eval gate. To answer most queries correctly, the retrieval+synthesis layer has to anchor relative dates against the timestamp of the turn they appear in.
